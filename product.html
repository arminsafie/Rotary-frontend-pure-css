The Aurum is the deck we build for people who notice when something's off — a hair of wow-and-flutter, a rumble under the bass. We isolate the DC motor from the plinth with a soft-coupled belt, hand-balance every platter to within half a gram, and align each tonearm on a two-point protractor before it ever plays a record.

It ships fully assembled with a pre-fitted moving-magnet cartridge, an anti-static mat, and a spare belt. Setup takes about ten minutes: level it, drop the counterweight, and you're listening.
